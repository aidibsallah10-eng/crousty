## REEL 1 — Bienvenue / Brand Awareness

> **But** : Présenter le restaurant, créer une identité forte

### Prompt (copiez tout le texte ci-dessous)

```
Cinematic vertical Instagram Reel advertisement (9:16 ratio) for a street food restaurant called "CROUSTY BOWLS" in Geneva, Switzerland.

SCENE: Start with a dramatic close-up of a large black bowl filled with golden crispy chicken pieces, white rice, drizzled with white sauce and sweet Thai sauce, with crispy onions on top. Steam rises gently. The bowl is placed on a modern dark counter. Camera slowly pulls back revealing the restaurant interior: bright green walls, warm Edison bulb pendant lights hanging from ceiling, sleek modern serving counter with glass display. The atmosphere is lively, urban, and appetizing.

TEXT OVERLAYS appearing one by one:
- "CROUSTY" in large bold white grunge brush font
- "BOWLS" in large bold orange (#F7941D) grunge font
- "Genève • Plainpalais" in smaller white text
- "Frais • Généreux • Minute" in lime green (#8CC63F)
- "@croustybowls" in white at the bottom

COLOR PALETTE: Deep black (#0a0a0a) background, vibrant orange (#F7941D), lime green (#8CC63F), bright white.

STYLE: High-energy street food advertisement, cinematic slow motion, dramatic lighting, warm Edison bulb glow, urban grunge aesthetic, bold typography, paint splatter effects, fast-cut editing.

MOOD: Exciting, appetizing, young, urban, Gen Z energy. Like a luxury fast food brand reveal.

End with the Crousty Bowls logo (crown icon + CROUSTY BOWLS text in orange and white) centered on black background with a subtle splatter effect.

No voiceover. Add upbeat trap/hip-hop background music energy vibe.
```

## REEL 2 — Menu des 4 Bowls

> **But** : Montrer les 4 bowls au choix, donner envie

### Prompt (copiez tout le texte ci-dessous)

```
Fast-paced vertical Instagram Reel (9:16 ratio) food advertisement for "CROUSTY BOWLS" restaurant, Geneva, Switzerland. Formule: 10 CHF.

SEQUENCE OF 4 SHOTS (each 2-3 seconds), rapid-cut style:

SHOT 1 - CLASSIC BOWL: Extreme close-up macro shot of a black bowl with crispy golden fried chicken pieces on white rice, drizzled with creamy white sauce and red sweet Thai sauce, topped with crispy onions and chopped parsley. Camera slowly zooms in. Text: "CLASSIC" in bold orange grunge font. Satisfying crunch sound effect.

SHOT 2 - CORDON BLEU BOWL: Close-up of a black bowl with golden crispy cordon bleu pieces on white rice, white cream sauce drizzled over, crispy onions, parsley. Text: "CORDON BLEU" in bold lime green grunge font.

SHOT 3 - POISSON BOWL: Close-up of a black bowl with golden crispy fish fillet pieces on white rice, white sauce and sweet Thai sauce drizzled, crispy onions, parsley. Text: "POISSON" in bold pink/magenta grunge font.

SHOT 4 - VEGGIE BOWL: Close-up of a black bowl with golden crispy falafel balls on white rice, white sauce drizzled, crispy onions, fresh parsley. Text: "VEGGIE" in bold blue grunge font.

FINAL SHOT: All 4 bowls arranged together on a dark surface, top-down aerial view. Big orange text appears: "4 BOWLS AU CHOIX — FORMULE 10 CHF". Below: lime green text "1L HIT TEA OFFERT". Bottom bar in solid orange: "@croustybowls | #teamgourmand"

STYLE: High-energy food porn cinematic, slow motion sauce drizzle moments, dramatic lighting with rim light, dark moody background, bold grunge typography with paint splatter effects, orange and black color palette.

MOOD: Hungry-making, bold, street food luxury, Gen Z Instagram aesthetic.
```

## REEL 3 — Promo Formule 10 CHF

> **But** : Vendre la formule, urgence, appel à l'action

### Prompt (copiez tout le texte ci-dessous)

```
High-impact vertical Instagram Reel advertisement (9:16 ratio) for "CROUSTY BOWLS" street food restaurant, Geneva, Switzerland.

OPENING (0-2s): Black screen. Sudden flash of orange light. Bold white text SLAMS onto screen: "FORMULE DU JOUR" with electric spark effect.

MAIN SHOT (2-8s): Cinematic slow-motion close-up of a large black bowl overflowing with crispy golden chicken, white rice, thick white sauce being poured slowly from above in super slow motion — the sauce hits the chicken and spreads luxuriously. Steam rises. Dramatic side lighting. Orange color grading. 

Camera rotates 360 degrees around the bowl showing every angle. Then cuts to a Tetra Pak carton of iced tea (Hit Tea) slamming onto the counter next to the bowl with a satisfying thud and splash of liquid.

TEXT OVERLAYS with bold impact animation:
- Giant orange "10" takes up 70% of screen
- "CHF" in white next to it
- "= 1 BOWL + 1L HIT TEA" in lime green
- "FAIT MAISON" badge spinning in
- "PRÉPARÉ À LA COMMANDE" badge
- "Rue de Carouge 69 — Genève" in white
- "022 320 47 07" in orange

FINAL FRAME (last 2s): Solid orange background. Black bold text: "@croustybowls". White text: "#teamgourmand". Crown emoji icon above the text.

STYLE: Hype food commercial, Dutch angle camera, dramatic slow motion, neon-grunge aesthetic, bold kinetic typography, paint splatter transitions, orange and black color palette, high contrast.

MOOD: FOMO (fear of missing out), urgency, mouthwatering, street food hype. Energy like a Supreme or Nike ad but for food.

No dialogue. Add bass-heavy trap beat or aggressive hip-hop instrumental.
```

## Alternative : CapCut avec vos vraies photos

Si l'IA vidéo ne donne pas un résultat parfait, utilisez **CapCut** (gratuit sur iPhone) :

1. Ouvrez CapCut → Nouveau projet
2. Importez vos photos de bowls (Classic, Poisson, Falafel...)
3. Template à chercher : **"Food Reel"** ou **"Restaurant Ad"**
4. Remplacez le texte avec :
   - Titre : `CROUSTY BOWLS`
   - Sous-titre : `Formule 10 CHF — 1 Bowl + 1L Hit Tea`
   - Couleurs : Orange `#F7941D` · Vert `#8CC63F`
   - Handle : `@croustybowls`
   - Hashtags : `#teamgourmand #croustybowls #geneve`
